ABOUT KALA NIDHI

Preserving Heritage, Empowering Artisans

Kala Nidhi is a digital marketplace created to connect India's talented rural artisans with customers around the world.

We believe every handmade product carries a story, tradition, and the skill of the person who created it. Our platform helps artisans showcase their work, reach new customers, and build sustainable livelihoods.

From traditional handicrafts to contemporary handmade creations, Kala Nidhi brings India's rich craftsmanship closer to everyone.

🎨 Our Mission

To digitally empower rural artisans and preserve India's traditional craftsmanship.

🤝 Our Vision

A world where every artisan has the opportunity to showcase and sell their craft.

🧵Our Purpose

Connecting authentic Indian craftsmanship with customers through technology.
